# Landing page

Mode: Persuade
Primary target: `apps/web/app/page.tsx`
Audience: the initial operator — a serious job seeker running a high-volume, evidence-sensitive search across many tabs.
Job: understand what Pursuit is, believe the control model, inspect the actual workspace, and start a search.
Proof: the shipped product UI and honest capability states; no customer logos, testimonials, metrics, pricing, or submission claims.
Constraints: exact brand H1/CTA copy, WCAG 2.2 AA, 44px targets, reduced motion, no fake success, no copied Jobmate assets or layout.

## Chosen direction

A calm career-operations cockpit with the energy of a live product stage. Warm-white canvas, ink type, an electric ultramarine field and a chartreuse signal; Geist Sans/Mono; a real, tabbed Pursuit workspace inside a browser frame; four scroll-linked workflow states; asymmetric product bento; an interactive approval demonstration that explicitly performs no external action.

Memorable moment: the cobalt product stage begins slightly dimensional, flattens as it enters view, and the workspace stays inspectable while the page moves around it.

Unresolved: real model runtime, pricing, and production auth remain open product decisions and are labelled as such.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A calm operations cockpit, not an editorial case file. The product workspace itself carries the page inside a warm-white, cobalt, and chartreuse field; refuse the beige-paper + serif + stamp rut and the generic three-card SaaS grid.
OWN-WORLD: Warm white #fafaf9, ink #101012, electric ultramarine #3b32d6, chartreuse #d9f36a; Geist and Geist Mono; 14–28px radii, soft offset shadows, pill statuses, dense 10–12px product UI; trajectory mark, dotted coordinate grids, white app surfaces.
STORY: The visitor sees an inspectable Pursuit workspace, understands proposal-only control, walks four records from profile to timeline, checks capability truth, and starts a search.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Floating sticky header; centered live status pill; exact 72px H1; 18px subhead; paired CTA; trust line; two floating real product fragments; below, a full-bleed cobalt stage with a browser-framed tabbed workspace that flattens on scroll.
FORM: Brief-pinned direction, position 1, no roll: Jobmate's product-showcase energy translated through the Pursuit spec and palette; no copied assets, copy, or layout.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
