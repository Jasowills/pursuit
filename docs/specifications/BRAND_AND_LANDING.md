# Pursuit — Brand and landing page

## Brand status

Working name **Pursuit**. Do not register a domain, ship to stores or assert exclusivity until trademark, domain, product-name and store-name checks. Suggested verbal identity: precise, optimistic, calm, capable; no hype or promises of interviews.
Tagline: **Your next move, already in motion.**
Hero H1: **Your next opportunity doesn't wait. Neither should you.**
Subhead: **Find better roles, tailor every application, reach the right people, and keep your job search moving. Even when you're offline.**
Primary CTA: **Start your search**. Secondary: **See how it works**.
Logo direction: custom simple north-east trajectory/arrow mark, not a copied Jobmate icon. Lowercase wordmark `pursuit.` in bold Geist. Verify distinctiveness before finalizing.

## Landing IA

1. Sticky minimal header: wordmark left; Product, How it works, Features, FAQ center/right; Sign in text; black Get started button.
2. Hero: centered eyebrow with small sparkle and 1px gray border; H1; subhead; paired CTA; trust note: 'You approve what gets sent.'
3. Product showcase: genuine Pursuit app screenshot (not decorative fake data in production), within bordered rounded browser frame; overview KPIs, agent activity, jobs pipeline.
4. How it works: Connect your career profile -> Discover and qualify -> Review and approve -> Track every outcome.
5. Features: discovery, page watchers, browser agent, tailored resume vault, outreach, mobile sync.
6. Safety and control: approvals, transparent history, no invented qualifications.
7. Mobile feature section: paired phone views and desktop dashboard.
8. FAQ: platforms, Gmail permissions, browser support, pricing TBD, data handling, application limits.
9. Final CTA, legal footer and links.

Gap eyebrow->H1 24px; H1->subhead 24px; subhead->CTA 32px; CTA->preview 88px. H1 max width 960px, 68px/1.05, -0.045em, weight 700; subhead max width 650px, 18px/1.6. CTA height 48px, radius 10px. Preview width min(1100px,100%), 1px #E4E4E7 border, radius 20px, subtle shadow 0 18px 60px rgba(17,17,17,.08); top offset 0, no floating 3D.
Mobile baseline 390px: 20px gutters, header 64px, hero top 64px, H1 42px/1.1 max width 360px, CTA stacked full width or wrapped, preview after 56px; render a purpose-built mobile app view, never illegibly shrink desktop screenshot.

## Motion

150-200ms transitions for hover/focus, ease-out. Optional one-time subtle preview fade/translate (8px) with reduced-motion disabled. Avoid autoplay-heavy animation.

## Copy guidelines

Plain, direct, confidence without guaranteed outcomes. Never claim auto-submission when the product requires final approval. Make safety controls visible above fold or near hero.
