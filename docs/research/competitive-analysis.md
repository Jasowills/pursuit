# Career automation competitive research

Initial official-source review: **2026-10-21**; core Simplify, Teal, LoopCV, Jobright, Huntr, Rezi, LazyApply and Sonara pages rechecked **2026-10-22**. This is a point-in-time product-documentation review, not a hands-on benchmark. Features, coverage, safety, volume and outcome statements below are **vendor claims**. Unknown means the reviewed sources do not settle the question; it must not be presented as an absent feature. Prices and quantitative hiring-success promises are deliberately excluded because they are not needed for this product decision.

## Decision

Pursuit should compete on trustworthy execution of a person's career strategy: an inspectable CareerIdentity, source-backed writing, controlled browser actions, correspondence and durable records. Autofill, resume tailoring, tracking and even autonomous submission are already competitive requirements. Calling Pursuit an agent or adding a mobile screen is insufficient differentiation.

## Strengths and meaningful boundaries

**Simplify is the closest execution reference.** Copilot and Autopilot explicitly distinguish assisted review from delegated submission. Autopilot's setup documentation also describes iOS use. At review time, asking the user an unanswered screening question and scheduled automatic applications were described as upcoming; current behavior was to fail an uncertain application. Do not cite those roadmap items as shipped. [Getting started](https://help.simplify.jobs/help/articles/1784339-getting-started-with-autopilot). Its mobile instructions describe Safari autofill and supported-job Autopilot submission, including automatically saved tailored documents. That is already a mobile application workflow, not merely a tracker. [iOS flow](https://help.simplify.jobs/en/articles/6916710-browsing-saving-and-applying-to-jobs-in-the-app).

**Teal and Huntr are strong continuity references.** Teal links a resume version and contacts to a job, making later follow-up contextual. Its documented template process hands work to the user's inbox. Contacts include relationships, goals and follow-up dates. [Contacts guide](https://help.tealhq.com/en/articles/9509581-getting-started-contacts-tracker). Teal's current privacy policy additionally describes Google Contacts/Calendar context for career recommendations and read-only access; it should not be characterized as a purely disconnected manual CRM. [Privacy policy](https://www.tealhq.com/privacy-policy). Huntr likewise supports contacts attached to one or several opportunities. [Contact guide](https://guides.huntr.co/Managing-Contacts-81a28d867dac4d2c824e94deb64ffd2e?pvs=25).

**LoopCV offers a useful operational vocabulary.** Its knowledge base distinguishes submitted forms, sent emails, extension-supported jobs, external manual applications, submitting and failed states. It documents optional paid SMTP and otherwise forwarded replies from a managed address. Login/account requirements can force manual work. These are important boundaries to expose instead of collapsing every attempt into “applied.” [Knowledge base](https://loopcv.freshdesk.com/support/solutions/articles/103000399849-knowledge-base).

**Jobright spans discovery through execution.** Its profile-driven autofill and autonomous Agent are separate advertised paths. The public Agent page supports the existence of the auto-apply claim but does not establish exactly when a user reviews a generated artifact. That is an evidence gap, not proof that reviews are impossible.

**Rezi has expanded beyond document generation.** The June 2026 extension guide documents LinkedIn import, ATS autofill, tracking and interview practice. Describing it as “only a resume builder” would be outdated. Its resume-first workflow is a clear lesson for reducing setup friction while making source quality visible.

**LazyApply prioritizes execution and referrals in its messaging.** Its “profiles will never get blocked” claim is marketing, not evidence of platform-policy compliance or a technical guarantee. Pursuit should make bounded claims about supported workflows and measured results instead. No independent assessment of vendor deliverability, application acceptance or interview outcomes was performed.

## Implications for Pursuit

These are proposed product choices, not claims that all competitors lack them:

1. **CareerIdentity as evidence, not just a profile.** Import resume, work history and writing samples, then preserve source, confidence, confirmation and dates for each claim. Let the user resolve contradictions once. Show which source supports a generated sentence and prevent a keyword request from silently becoming a claimed skill.
2. **Personal writing as a reviewed capability.** Learn voice from user-approved samples, maintain a compact editable voice profile and show substantive factual changes. Resume tailoring, cover letters, application answers and outreach should draw on the same identity without inventing metrics.
3. **An execution harness with visible boundaries.** Separate prepare, fill, review and submit. Give each operation an artifact, status, reason for stopping and recoverable checkpoint. Submission authority should be explicit, scoped and revocable. Unknown facts, login, verification and unusual questions should enter a clear user-action queue.
4. **Correspondence belongs to the opportunity.** Build Gmail drafts tied to a job/contact with recipient evidence, attached resume version and source context. Draft creation and sending require different authority. Preserve message IDs and user edits; a draft is not a sent email or a delivered application.
5. **Local-first must be inspectable.** Define exactly what stays on disk, what reaches model providers, which credentials live in the OS keychain and what syncs. Local-first does not mean offline AI or no external processing. Give export, deletion, audit history and redaction practical interfaces.
6. **Remote companion as a control surface.** Phone review, approval, corrections and pause/resume should control a desktop run with authenticated device pairing and expiring authority. Avoid implying the desktop can execute while asleep; queue commands and report connectivity honestly. Simplify's mobile execution means “has mobile” is not a defensible wedge.
7. **Optimize meaningful outcomes.** Measure verified completion, correction rate, relevant responses and time recovered. Submission counts alone reward low-quality volume. Keep failed attempts, drafted material, form completion and actual submission distinct.

## Validation needed before public competitive claims

Run the same authorized test applications through available products; inspect answer accuracy, editable review, failure recovery, exact submission evidence and privacy controls. Check mobile store listings and authenticated product flows before claiming device coverage. Review current privacy/security terms before making data-residency or training comparisons. Public documentation did not establish a comparable claim-level provenance model, local-first desktop architecture or Gmail-draft workflow across all vendors; those remain research unknowns rather than proven exclusive advantages.
