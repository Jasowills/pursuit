# Closing CTA reference study

Observed 2026-10-23 on the first-party production sites. The findings distinguish visible design observations from proposed Pursuit behavior. No source code or proprietary visual assets were copied.

## Linear

[Linear home](https://linear.app/), closing section above the footer:

- The final acquisition section follows customer evidence and reduces the page to a centered two-line heading with two compact actions.
- The live view uses a nearly black field, ample vertical space, a white primary action and a darker secondary action. It does not repeat its feature grid or explain implementation details.
- The directory footer begins beyond a thin divider. The ending works as a deliberate pause after the dense product walkthrough.

**Interpretation for Pursuit:** keep a single decision visible, shorten the prose and let the surrounding negative space supply emphasis. Pursuit should retain its own typography, mark and lime accent rather than imitate Linear’s page.

## Raycast

[Raycast home](https://www.raycast.com/), closing section above the footer:

- The closing CTA joins a large tactile keyboard illustration with a small centered acquisition block.
- One download action dominates. Hardware/OS prerequisites and alternate installation paths sit below it in quieter text.
- A red abstract brand form carries the page into its footer. The visual object, not an explanatory paragraph, supplies the memorable ending.

**Interpretation for Pursuit:** use an object connected to career work and make current availability explicit. A source alpha must not borrow the language of an available signed installer.

## Original direction: the search aperture

A quiet black stage with a concise centered invitation, for example “Make room for your next role.” A narrow horizontal aperture below it receives a few typographic job slips and aligns them into one orderly application folio. Slips represent illustrative roles, not live search results. The folio carries the Pursuit arrow and one legible status.

The composition should make the product’s purpose visible: scattered search work becomes a reviewed next step. Do not add a second dashboard or another résumé mockup to the closing section.

### Interaction constraints

- The primary acquisition action is visible and usable before any animation. Label it according to the current build state, such as “Get the desktop alpha”, with a source-build qualifier.
- A restrained pointer response or three-position keyboard-operable control can move between “Find”, “Prepare” and “Review”. This is an optional local visual preview, never a required drag gesture.
- Entering the viewport may play one short assembly motion. Do not loop fast motion or imply that a real application was sent.
- Reduced-motion preference shows the completed folio with no translating slips. Mobile uses the same completed object with adequate button targets and no horizontal overflow.
- Keep supporting copy to one sentence. Put technical setup guidance in the download section or documentation.
- No Companion promotion, fake counts, invented completion receipts, or implied universal macOS binary.

The combination is an original proposal for Pursuit. This study does not claim the concept has never appeared anywhere else.
